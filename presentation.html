01 · Problem Statement
The Epistemic Disconnect in Enterprise BI
Why Traditional BI & Naive GenAI Fail in Operations
Modern dashboards show symptoms, but cannot isolate causal provenance or enforce safety.
1. Dashboards Show What, Not Why
When hourly conversion drops 14% or gateway latency spikes 300%, traditional tools (Grafana, Tableau) display metric alarms but cannot discern if the cause is a code deployment, gateway outage, or ETL ingestion lag.
2. GenAI Hallucination Risk
Generic LLM chatbots guess root causes without empirical validation. They invent fictitious log citations, perform uncalibrated math, and propose dangerous multi-million dollar remediation actions.
3. Missing Zero-Trust Governance
Operators lack cryptographic provenance linking actions to verified records. Uncontrolled automated agents trigger destructive restarts without verifying user role entitlements or data confidentiality.
Root Problem: Lack of mathematical grounding and causal provenance in observability
